About

TIKOONZ TV is a Korean-based music talk platform where professors and industry professionals examine contemporary music — from Hip-Hop and EDM to K-POP.

Rather than simply presenting tracks or reacting to trends, TIKOONZ TV focuses on how music is constructed. Each episode explores musical structure, production choices, sonic references, and the cultural and historical environments that shape sound. Our approach values clarity and precision while maintaining depth, offering insight without sacrificing accessibility.

The platform is designed for listeners who want to move beyond surface-level consumption. Musicians, producers, students, educators, and deeply engaged fans use TIKOONZ TV as a space for critical listening and contextual understanding. We believe that meaningful engagement with music requires time, continuity, and informed discussion.

TIKOONZ TV operates as a curated channel rather than a collection of isolated clips. Episodes are sequenced to play continuously, encouraging long-form listening and immersion. This structure reflects our belief that context matters — that music is best understood not in fragments, but as part of a larger conversation.

At its core, TIKOONZ TV is an archive. In an environment where content is rapidly consumed and forgotten, we aim to build material that remains relevant over time. The platform documents how musical scenes evolve, how artists develop, and how listening practices change across generations and cultures.

By bridging academic perspectives with real-world industry experience, TIKOONZ TV positions itself as both a media platform and a knowledge resource. As the archive grows, the site becomes a living map of contemporary music culture — curated, structured, and built for long-term engagement.
