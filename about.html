About

TIKOONZ TV is a Korean-based music talk platform where professors and industry professionals examine contemporary music — from Hip-Hop and EDM to K‑POP.

We don’t simply introduce tracks. We unpack structure, production choices, sonic references, and the cultural context behind the sound. Each episode is designed to be informative, precise, and enjoyable — without losing depth.

Our goal is to build an archive that can be revisited: a place where conversations remain valuable after the moment passes. In a landscape driven by speed, we focus on continuity — documenting how scenes evolve, how artists develop, and how audiences listen.

TIKOONZ TV is also a bridge between academia and the field. We bring analysis, history, and critical listening into direct conversation with industry insights — creating a format that can speak to fans, creators, and professionals alike.

Shows are curated and sequenced to play continuously, like a channel rather than a playlist. As the library grows, the site becomes not just a collection, but a living map of contemporary music culture.
